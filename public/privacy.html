1. Who we are

PsaOnline (operating as Psa Accessories Online, "we", "us", or "our") is an e-commerce shop specializing in Gen-Z aesthetic streetwear accessories, Y2K silver chrome jewelry, shades, and bags based in Phnom Penh, Cambodia.

By creating an account, browsing our catalog, or placing an order, you agree to this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use our services.

2. Information we collect

We collect only the personal information required to process and deliver your orders and manage your account:

  • Account details: Your full name, email address, phone number or Telegram contact, and password hash.
  • Delivery details: Your physical delivery address in Phnom Penh, delivery notes, and optional precise map location coordinates (latitude and longitude) selected via the checkout map pin.
  • Profile photo: An optional profile photo avatar uploaded to personalize your account.
  • Payment verification slips: Screenshots or image files of transfer slips uploaded to verify Bakong KHQR or ABA mobile bank transfers.
  • Order history: Items ordered, quantities, timestamps, order amounts in USD, selected delivery options, and fulfillment status.
  • Customer communications: Any details you share when contacting our customer support team via Telegram or web form.

3. Where your data is stored

Your persistent account data, order histories, uploaded transfer slips, and product databases are securely hosted in our dedicated PostgreSQL database managed on Supabase.

The application web service and API servers are hosted and deployed via Render in Singapore.

4. Browser storage & cookies

To give you a seamless shopping experience without intrusive advertising trackers, certain non-sensitive shopping preferences are saved directly and exclusively in your own browser's local storage:

  • Shopping bag / Cart: Items currently held in your cart.
  • Wishlist / Saved items: Accessories you marked with a heart.
  • Recently viewed: Accessories you viewed during your session.
  • Preferences: Your selected interface theme (Light or Dark mode).
  • Saved checkout addresses: Pre-filled delivery address information saved for faster checkout on your device.

This data remains on your physical device and is never sold or used for cross-site tracking. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

5. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your buyer account and secure login sessions.
  • To process, confirm, pack, and deliver your accessories to your doorstep in Phnom Penh.
  • To verify bank payments by comparing transfer slips against incoming transactions.
  • To contact you by phone or Telegram regarding order delivery, status changes, or address clarification.
  • To comply with Cambodian accounting, tax, and legal requirements.
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent orders, duplicate slip submissions, or platform misuse.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

6. Payments & bank privacy

PsaOnline supports Bakong Universal KHQR, ABA Pay, and Cash on Delivery (Phnom Penh only).

We never collect or store credit/debit card numbers or bank login credentials. All digital payments occur within your own banking application (ABA Mobile, ACLEDA, Wing, Canadia, Bakong, etc.). When you upload a transfer slip, we only view the transaction reference, sender name, timestamp, and amount required to confirm payment.

7. Who we share it with

  • Delivery drivers & couriers: We share your recipient name, phone number, delivery address, notes, and pin location so couriers can deliver your order safely.
  • Payment verification staff: Authorized internal staff review uploaded transfer slips to mark orders as verified.
  • Legal authorities: Only when legally required by Cambodian law, regulations, or court orders.

8. Other services loaded

To provide high performance and security, our web pages load code from trusted CDN providers:

  • Tailwind CSS CDN & unpkg: Used for responsive typography and icons.
  • OpenStreetMap & Leaflet: Interactive maps rendered at checkout for precise location pin selection.
  • Unsplash: Product imagery and aesthetic photography assets.
  • QR Server: Used to dynamically generate KHQR scannable codes at checkout.

9. How long we keep data

We retain order histories, receipts, and invoices for as long as necessary to fulfill the order, honor customer support requests, and satisfy Cambodian commercial recordkeeping standards.

Uploaded bank slip screenshots are retained only as long as needed to confirm payment and resolve any disputes.

10. Your choices & rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and review the personal information we hold about you.
  • Update or correct your phone number, name, or password via the Account Settings page.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal records by contacting support.
  • Clear your local cart, wishlist, and theme preferences at any time through your browser settings.

11. Security

All connections to PsaOnline are encrypted over HTTPS (TLS). Passwords are cryptographically hashed using standard Bcrypt algorithms before storage. We advise choosing a strong, unique password and keeping your login credentials confidential.

12. Governing law

This Privacy Policy is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia. Any dispute arising out of or related to this policy shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the competent courts of Cambodia.
